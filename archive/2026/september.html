STUDENT EXPERIENCE

A team of UC Santa Cruz students and their professor are doing rapid-response research to measure how giant kelp forests respond to ocean warming expected during the  “super El Niño” that is now unfolding. Their real-time study aims to deliver actionable insights to protect coastal marine ecosystems. Community members can sustain their work with donations that would support student training.

IMPACTFUL EVENTS

OCTOBER 13  | 5:30–8:30 p.m. | MENLO PARK, CA
Kraw Lecture: Could a "Super El Niño" be coming?
UC Santa Cruz researchers are closely tracking the development of a potential “super El Niño” this year. In this Kraw Lecture, two UC Santa Cruz experts will explore the natural forces that drive El Niño events, how scientists forecast major climate shifts, and what current research can tell us about the months ahead. While every El Niño is unique, decades of research at UC Santa Cruz provide valuable insights into potential impacts and how communities can prepare.

Fueling the biotech flywheel with fundamental discoveries and driven graduates

UC Santa Cruz is strategically elevating its engagement with the biotechnology sector to emphasize its essential role in producing fundamental research and future talent. By attending and hosting major international forums, the campus is forging direct connections between its researchers and industry leaders, while bridging the gap between basic lab discoveries and real-world medical applications.

Science Communication I: Sharing Science With a General Audience

This new online course will teach the importance of science communication and give a general overview of the field. It will cover how to determine and reach your audience with various formats of communication, such as journalism, media relations, and social media, among others. This course is designed for those with a background in science, research, and/or communications and is the foundation of an exciting new Science Communication certificate program offered through UCSC Silicon Valley Extention.

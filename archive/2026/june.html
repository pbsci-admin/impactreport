RNA Center founder Harry Noller cements a legacy of brilliance and creativity

While the scientific community has historically focused on DNA as the primary blueprint of life, a pioneering biochemist at the UC Santa Cruz, proved that RNA is far more than a passive messenger. Harry Noller, founder of the campus's Center for Molecular Biology of RNA, spent nearly six decades demonstrating that RNA actively powers the ribosome.

SEYMOUR MARINE DISCOVERY CENTER
Seymour Center's New Exhibit: See More HQ 

Visit the Seymour Marine Discovery Center and check out See More HQ, a new bilingual exhibit with touchscreens showing games, maps and immersive videos of regional scientific research and solutions. See More HQ is the center's first new permanent exhibit in 15 years and brings real-time UC Santa Cruz coastal research to the public.
